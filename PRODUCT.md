# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The portfolio serves a mixed audience: recruiters and hiring managers evaluating fit, fellow developers inspecting craft and implementation judgment, and potential collaborators or clients looking for proof of execution. The site should prioritize a fast, confident read of Harshit Gulati's strengths while rewarding deeper exploration through projects, components, and writing.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio exists to make Harshit memorable as a software engineer, builder, and sharer. Success means visitors quickly understand what he builds, why he thinks clearly, and where to go next: inspect case studies, try components, read articles, or make contact.

## Brand Personality

Bold, builder-minded, precise. The voice should feel human and opinionated without becoming casual to the point of gimmick. The interface should feel modern and distinctive, with enough restraint that the work remains the focus.

## Anti-references

Avoid template portfolio patterns, corporate brochure styling, generic SaaS landing-page polish, and overly playful visual systems. Motion should not feel performative, decorative, or distracting.

## Design Principles

- Lead with a clear point of view, not a resume summary.
- Show craft through working examples, case studies, and writing rather than claiming it.
- Make exploration obvious for every audience without flattening the site into a link list.
- Use personality in copy, motion, and interaction while keeping the interface disciplined.
- Treat dark mode, accessibility, and performance as part of the craft signal.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-friendly navigation, visible focus states, reduced-motion alternatives, and readable light and dark themes. Interactive demos and copy actions should remain usable without relying on color or motion alone.
